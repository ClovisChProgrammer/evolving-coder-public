## English

### What is Frequency Portrait?

An immersive adaptive journey that discovers the exact binaural frequency your brain resonates with. Unlike simple quizzes, it uses **binary search** to narrow from a wide scan to your precise sweet spot, with real-time audio visualization, guided breathing, and multi-dimensional rating.

### How to Use

1. Open `frequency-portrait.html` in any modern browser
2. Put on **stereo headphones** (required for binaural beats)
3. Click **Begin Journey**

### The Journey

#### Phase 1 — Baseline (30 seconds)
Close your eyes and breathe with the animated circle. We measure your resting state before testing begins.

#### Phase 2 — Wide Scan (6 tests × 60 seconds)
You test 6 evenly spaced frequencies (2, 6, 8, 10, 13, 20 Hz). Each test:
- **Listen** for 60 seconds with real-time waveform visualizer
- **Breathe** with the guided 4-4-4-4 pacer
- **Rate** three dimensions: Mood, Energy, Focus (1–4 scale each)

#### Phase 3 — Adaptive Refinement
Based on your initial ratings, the algorithm identifies your top frequency and tests neighboring frequencies to find your exact sweet spot.

### The 3-Axis Rating System

| Axis | What It Measures | Scale |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| Mood | Emotional state — calm, happy, elevated | 1–4 |
| Energy | Activation level — relaxed to alert | 1–4 |
| Focus | Mental clarity — scattered to sharp | 1–4 |

The system auto-advances when you've rated all three axes, or you can skip.

### Results Dashboard

- **Resonant Frequency**: Your brain's ideal Hz with brain state label
- **Spectrum Response**: Bar chart showing your response across all frequencies
- **Multi-Axis Radar**: Visual profile across mood, energy, focus, calm
- **Personalized Recommendations**: Best frequencies for mood, energy, focus + frequencies to avoid
- **Session History**: Track how your profile evolves (saved in localStorage)

### Soundscape Generator

Generate a personalized binaural WAV file based on your results:
- **15 min**: Quick session
- **30 min**: Standard session
- **60 min**: Deep session

The WAV uses your resonant frequency with a 200 Hz carrier, fade in/out, and stereo panning.

### Tips

- **Be honest** with ratings — no wrong answers
- **Minimize distractions** during tests
- **Breathe slowly** with the guide
- **Don't overthink** — first instinct is usually accurate
- **Retake periodically** — your profile evolves
- **Use the soundscape** to experience your ideal frequency
